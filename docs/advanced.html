Advanced kit

A short look at what's out there for the next big step. This isn't a shopping list: by the time you need advanced kit, you'll know what you like better than any guide can tell you.

Don't rush this. Intermediate is the highest grade I recommend buying. As you gain experience your preferences change, so choose top-end kit yourself, once your draw weight and draw length have been stable for a season.

What changes at the top level

Rough prices from UK shops, checked 14 November 2026:

  • Risers: about £600–£1,000, for example the W&W Wiawis ATF-X, Gillo GT, Hoyt Xceed 2 and Uukha Xpro3. Try several in the shop: grip and balance matter more than brand.
  • Limbs: about £725–£1,000, for example Uukha U5 and W&W Wiawis MXT or NS. Buy them only once your draw weight has stopped going up.
  • Sights: about £265–£460, for example the Shibuya Ultima RC3, RC4 and RC Pro, or the Axcel Achieve.
  • Pressure buttons: about £95–£135, for example the Beiter, AAE Gold and Fivics Airex. The Shibuya DX is still good enough for most archers.
  • Stabilisers: complete sets cost about £330–£500, for example the W&W HMC Plus and ACS-15.

Together, that's roughly £2,000–£3,100 before arrows, tab and case.

Arrows: a carbon-aluminium second set

Keep your aluminium arrows for club shooting, and consider carbon-aluminium arrows as a second set. The cheapest I know of is Merlin's Alimax. The next step up is the Pandarus CA320.

Be careful with shaft charts. They are quite inaccurate for these arrows. Before you buy, talk to other archers who shoot them at a similar draw weight and arrow length.
