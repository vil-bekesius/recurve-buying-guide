Advanced kit

A short look at what's out there for the next big step. This isn't a shopping list: by the time you need advanced kit, you'll know what you like better than any guide can tell you.

Don't rush this. Intermediate is the highest grade I recommend buying. As you gain experience your preferences change, so choose top-end kit yourself, once your draw weight and draw length have been stable for a season.

What changes at the top level

  • Risers: for example the W&W Wiawis ATF-X, Gillo GT, Hoyt Xceed 2 and Uukha Xpro3. Try several in the shop: grip and balance matter more than brand.
  • Limbs: for example Uukha U5 and W&W Wiawis MXT or NS. Buy them only once your draw weight has stopped going up.
  • Sights: for example the Shibuya Ultima RC3, RC4 and RC Pro, or the Axcel Achieve.
  • Pressure buttons: for example the Beiter, AAE Gold and Fivics Airex. The Shibuya DX is still good enough for most archers.
  • Stabilisers: complete sets, for example the W&W HMC Plus and ACS-15.
