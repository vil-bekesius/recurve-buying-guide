Starter kit

Your first bow of your own: about £400–£540 for a complete Olympic recurve kit on an ILF riser. It should last a year or more, and the riser can stay with you when you move to stronger limbs.

Buy ILF, not a bolt-on training bow. ILF (International Limb Fitting) limbs fit nearly every ILF riser, so you can keep the riser and change limbs as your draw weight goes up. Avoid one-piece or bolt-on training kits such as Merlin's Adult Olympic Kit (Core Shift riser): you can't reuse their parts and they are hard to tune.

Who this is for

  • You've finished a beginners course and shoot at least weekly.
  • Your draw length is consistent and your coach agrees you are ready.
  • Your draw weight is still rising, so you want cheap limbs and arrows that you'll replace within a year.

Where to spend and where to save

  • Spend on the riser. Archery GB's Start Archery guide suggests putting most of the budget into the riser and keeping first limbs to about £100 or less.
  • Save on limbs and arrows. You'll replace both as your draw weight rises.
  • Stabilisers can wait. A single long rod is enough at starter draw weights; add a V-bar and side rods later.
