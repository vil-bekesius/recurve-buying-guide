Intermediate kit

For the archer who has shot for one to three years and enters club competitions: about £630–£1,210 for a better riser, carbon limbs, full stabilisers, a clicker and carbon arrows. You can carry over your rest, quiver, stand, bag and other accessories from your starter kit.

Who this is for

  • You shoot two or three times a week and enter club or county shoots.
  • You're working towards Archery GB classifications (Archer, Bowman and Master Bowman tiers).
  • Your draw weight is still rising, but more slowly than in your first year.

What changes from a starter kit

ArrowsAluminiumCarbon, matched for spine and weight
String, tab, sightBasicModern 8125-type string, plate-and-ledge tab, carbon sight extension, tunable button
When to add a clicker and full stabilisers: ask your coach. Most archers add a clicker after three to six months of consistent shooting, and add a V-bar and side rods as their draw weight goes up. Archery GB's stabiliser guide suggests a long rod at or just below your draw length.

Packages

Few UK shops sell a complete intermediate Olympic package. Chiltern's Hoyt Podium 25" Intermediate Package (£849.95) has the riser and limbs but no sight, stabilisers or clicker, and comes with aluminium arrows. Building your own usually gives better value.
