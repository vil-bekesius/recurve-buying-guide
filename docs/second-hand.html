Buying second-hand

Second-hand kit is an option, but proceed with caution. Do your research before you buy, and only buy from reputable second-hand sources.

Check the new price first. Second-hand kit is often not as cheap as you'd think, once you add shipping and remember that it comes with no warranty. A new part with a warranty can be the better deal (buy once, cry once).

Where to buy

  • Your club. Members often sell their old kit, and you can see it before you buy.
  • sellmybow.com, a UK marketplace for used archery equipment.

What to pay

For kit in good condition, I'd expect to pay around two-thirds of the new price.

Risers and limbs

  • Risers: a used riser is an option, but check it carefully, or ask someone experienced to check it for you.
  • Limbs: only buy used limbs if you can inspect them before you pay, to confirm there is no significant damage.

Buying safely

  • Payment: check what buyer protection your payment method gives before you pay.
  • Set-up: set up the bow before you shoot it. If you are unsure, pay one of the recommended shops to set it up and check it.
  • Second opinion: ask an experienced club member or coach to look at a part before you buy it.
