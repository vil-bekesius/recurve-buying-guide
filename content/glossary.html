Glossary

Terms used in this guide that a beginners' course may not have covered.

Brace height
The distance from the string to the deepest part of the grip when the bow is strung.
Centre shot
How far left or right the arrow sits on the rest, relative to the string and the centre of the bow.
Extender
A short rod between the riser and the V-bar that moves the stabilisers forward.
Limb bolts
The bolts in the limb pockets that hold the limbs. Winding them in or out changes the draw weight slightly.
Limb pocket
The slot at each end of the riser that a limb fits into.
Nocking point
The marked place on the string where the arrow clips on.
Plunger
Another name for the pressure button.
Spine
How stiff an arrow shaft is.
V-bar
The bracket that holds the two side rods at an angle.
