My three rules:
  1. Buy sooner rather than later, if your budget allows. A bow set up for you shoots better, is more fun, and gets you shooting more often.
  2. Buy once, cry once. Start on a CNC-machined intermediate riser: it lasts a long way into your archery career.
  3. Aluminium first, then aluminium-carbon. Start on aluminium arrows, and move to aluminium-carbon with your second set of limbs. Avoid pure carbon: lost carbon arrows are hard to find on multi-purpose fields, and many clubs don't allow them.
