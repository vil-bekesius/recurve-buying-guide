Advanced kit

Kit for county, regional and national-level Olympic recurve: about £1,900–£2,000 for the parts that last, plus about £1,240–£1,720 for top limbs and arrows once your draw weight is settled.

Buy in two stages. Buy the riser, sight, button, clicker and stabilisers now: you don't grow out of them. Buy top limbs and X10-class arrows only when your draw weight has been the same for several months, because a change of a few pounds can mean a new spine. Merlin gives the same advice.

Who this is for

  • You shoot four or more times a week, or train to a plan with a coach.
  • Your draw weight and draw length have been stable for at least a season.
  • You shoot 70 m WA 720 and 1440 rounds and want kit that is consistent in wind.
