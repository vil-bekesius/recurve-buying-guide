Advanced kit

A short look at what's out there for the next big step. This isn't a shopping list: by the time you need advanced kit, you'll know what you like better than any guide can tell you.

Don't rush this. Intermediate is the highest grade I recommend buying. As you gain experience your preferences change, so choose top-end kit yourself, once your draw weight and draw length have been stable for a season.

What changes at the top level

  • Risers: the current top risers from W&W Wiawis (Meta DX, Meta LX, ATF-DX and ATF-EX) and Hoyt (Xceed 2, and the Formula SR if you choose Hoyt's Formula limb fitting). Try several in the shop: grip and balance matter more than brand.
  • Limbs: top-end limbs. Buy them only once your draw weight has stopped going up.
  • Sights: the Shibuya Ultima RC3 or RC4.
  • Pressure buttons: the Beiter button. The Shibuya DX is still good enough for most archers.
  • Stabilisers: the current RamRods (Ultra 4 and Vektor 2) and Shrewd (Revel and RevX) ranges.
